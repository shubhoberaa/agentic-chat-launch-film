# Agentic Chat launch film brief

- **Format:** 18-second product launch film; 1920×1080 at 60 fps, 120 BPM.
- **Audience/use:** product launch post and project landing page; designed to read muted, with score and restrained interface cues.
- **One idea:** one request can be split across coordinated agents and returned as a finished result.
- **Brand:** use the repository's Agentic Chat mark, blue accent, dark UI and Geist typography. The supplied blue gradient and mark are intentional brand assets.
- **Hard bans for this cut:** no stock footage, serif or italic display type, decorative particles, fake numbers, filler labels, neon/glow, or more than one accent hue. Keep all copy tied to the depicted task.
- **Output:** 16:9 MP4 master under 20 seconds; source remains editable in Remotion.
